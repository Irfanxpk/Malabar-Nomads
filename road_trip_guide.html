00
Friday Night · Departure

Clearing the Lowlands

Beat the traffic by departing in the evening. The Kondotty–Malappuram stretch is brutal by day — driving at night turns it into a clean highway run straight into the hills.

7:00 PM

Depart Kozhikode

Leave home. Take the standard route via Malappuram → Palakkad. Roughly 3.5–4 hours on NH-66 / NH-544. Night driving skips the notorious daytime congestion.

10:30 PM – 11:00 PM

Overnight in Palakkad or Pollachi

Check into a transit hotel in Palakkad town or push an extra 45 minutes to stay near Pollachi foothills for an early Saturday start. Pollachi stay recommended to be closest to the Aliyar Checkpost.

01
Saturday · Day 1

The 40 Hairpin Climb — Valparai

The most famous ghat road in Tamil Nadu. Forty continuous, perfectly engineered switchbacks rising through shola forest and tea estates into one of India's most pristine hill towns.

7:00 AM – 8:30 AM

Pollachi → Aliyar Checkpost

Early start is essential. Drive from Pollachi and clear the Aliyar Checkpost before 9 AM. Morning light through the forest is spectacular.

8:30 AM – 11:00 AM

The Ascent — All 40 Hairpins

Take your time on the climb. The bends are numbered; each one a reward. Keep the windows down. Watch for the endangered Nilgiri Tahr between bends 4 and 11.

12:00 PM

Check In · Valparai

Check into a tea bungalow or forest homestay. Freshen up before the afternoon exploration loop.

2:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Afternoon Exploration

Sholayar Dam and Nallamudi Viewpoint are the afternoon highlights. Both require minimal effort for maximum payoff.

Evening

Mist Drive through Estate Roads

Valparai's estate roads are completely uncommercialized. As mist rolls in at dusk, drive the backroads through Tata Tea and Parry Agro estates slowly. This is the highlight many visitors miss.

🌊
SPOT 01 · 29th KM
Monkey Falls
Located right on the highway at the 29th km junction. A quick stop with a picturesque waterfall cutting through the roadside forest. Often visited by bonnet macaques — hence the name.
💡 Quick stop, 15 mins. Don't feed the monkeys — forest rules.
🏔️
SPOT 02 · 9TH HAIRPIN
Loam's View Point
Positioned at the 9th hairpin bend, this viewpoint offers a sweeping panorama of the Aliyar Dam reservoir glistening far below. On clear mornings, the entire plain up to Pollachi is visible.
💡 Best with morning light. Watch for Nilgiri Tahr on the adjacent cliff faces.
💧
SPOT 03 · AFTERNOON
Sholayar Dam
One of the highest arch dams in the region. A massive silent reservoir enclosed entirely by the Anamalai Tiger Reserve. The sheer scale of water surrounded by unbroken forest is humbling.
💡 Bring a jacket. Wind off the reservoir is strong year-round.
🌄
SPOT 04 · LATE AFTERNOON
Nallamudi Viewpoint
A short walk through green tea estates leads to a cliff-edge perch overlooking sheer valleys of the Anamalai Tiger Reserve. One of the more dramatic viewpoints in the entire Western Ghats range.
💡 Walk is 20–30 min each way. Sturdy shoes recommended; path can be slippery.
02
Sunday · Day 2

The 70 Hairpin Steering Workout — Kolli Hills

The most relentless mountain road in Tamil Nadu. Seventy hairpin bends built for precision — not a single straight to rest your wrists. The Eastern Ghats' best-kept driving secret.

6:00 AM – 11:30 AM

Early Checkout · Cross to Eastern Ghats

Descend Valparai's 40 bends gently (brakes will be hot — don't rush). Route: Pollachi → Kangeyam → Karur → Namakkal. This bypass entirely avoids Coimbatore's morning traffic.

11:30 AM – 1:00 PM

The Ascent — All 70 Hairpins

Turn off at Kalappanaickenpatti. Unlike Valparai's scenic pause-and-admire style, Kolli Hills demands focus. Seventy consecutive bends with a precision-built road surface. The most driver-focused ghat in South India.

1:30 PM – 3:30 PM

Agaya Gangai Falls · CRITICAL TIMING

Must arrive before 3 PM last entry. Approximately 1,000 steps descend into a deep jungle gorge to a 300-foot waterfall. Allow 1.5–2 hours for the round trip. Save energy — the climb back is steep.

4:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Temple + Sunset Viewpoints

Visit the ancient Arapaleeswarar Temple at the trailhead, then watch the Eastern Ghats turn golden at Seekuparai or the Kolli Hills Bend Viewpoint.

Night

Stay at Cliffside Resort

Kolli Hills has several secluded farm stays and cliffside resorts on the plateau. A very quiet, uncommercialized night ahead of tomorrow's big return sprint.

🌀
THE ROAD · 70 BENDS
The Kolli Hills Ghat Road
Built explicitly for precision. Seventy uninterrupted hairpins climbing through dense Eastern Ghats forest. The road surface is notably well-maintained, rewarding clean cornering technique.
💡 Descend in low gear, brakes get hot fast. Allow 30–40 minutes per direction.
💧
SPOT 05 · ⚠️ BEFORE 3 PM
Agaya Gangai Waterfalls
300-foot waterfall hidden at the bottom of a 1,000-step jungle gorge. The descent is intense; the falls, otherworldly. Save energy for the climb back — it's significantly harder.
⚠️ Last entry 3:00 PM sharp. Wear proper footwear. Carry water.
🛕
SPOT 06 · AT FALLS TRAILHEAD
Arapaleeswarar Temple
A 1,000+ year-old Shiva temple located at the trailhead of Agaya Gangai falls. One of the most peaceful, uncrowded ancient temples in the Eastern Ghats — visit before or after the waterfall trek.
💡 Dress modestly. No entry fee. Offers wonderful quiet after the waterfall crowds.
🌅
SPOT 07 · SUNSET
Seekuparai Viewpoint
A quiet, pristine viewpoint on the Eastern Ghats escarpment. Undiscovered by mainstream tourism, offering unobstructed sunset views across the Namakkal plains below.
💡 Very few visitors at sunset. Bring binoculars for bird sightings.
03
Monday · Day 3 · Return Sprint

Yercaud's Panoramic Loop & the Great Highway Sprint

The finale. A quick morning loop through Yercaud's colonial coffee estates and viewpoints, then a clean, high-speed charge down NH-544 all the way back to Kozhikode before midnight.

7:00 AM – 9:30 AM

Kolli Hills → Salem → Yercaud

Early checkout. Descend 70 hairpins and head straight for Salem, then climb Yercaud's 20 wide sweeping hairpins. Total approx. 2.5 hours. Yercaud's bends are leisurely compared to yesterday.

9:30 AM – 2:00 PM

Yercaud Highlights Loop

Pagoda Point for misty morning views. Lady's Seat and Gent's Seat for dramatic valley overlooks directly above Salem city. Lunch at the lakeside followed by the 32 km Loop Road through old coffee estates.

2:30 PM

Descend to Salem · Sprint Begins

Leave Yercaud, descend to Salem. Fuel up completely before joining the NH-544 highway west.

3:30 PM – 11:30 PM

The Highway Sprint Home

Salem → Erode Bypass → Coimbatore Bypass → Palakkad → Kozhikode. A clean, uninterrupted 4-lane national highway run. Salem to Palakkad is ~3.5–4 hours. Palakkad to Kozhikode another ~3.5 hours. Comfortable midnight arrival.

11:00 PM – 11:30 PM

🏠 Home · Kozhikode

Arrive safely. Well within the midnight deadline. 130 hairpins conquered, three hill stations ticked, and the car's brakes thoroughly exercised.

🌊
SPOT 08 · MORNING
Pagoda Point (Pyramid Point)
On the eastern edge of the Yercaud plateau. Morning mist hangs over the neighboring village valleys below. One of the finest sunrise viewpoints in the Salem district.
💡 Arrive by 10 AM before the mist fully clears for the best atmosphere.
🏙️
SPOT 09 · MID-MORNING
Lady's Seat & Gent's Seat
Two rock overlooks perched directly above Salem city, connected by a short path. Looking straight down 1,200 feet onto a city of 1.5 million is a dizzying, extraordinary perspective.
💡 The Gent's Seat has the more dramatic sheer drop. Avoid weekends — gets crowded.
☕
SPOT 10 · LUNCH
Yercaud Lake & 32 km Loop Road
A slow drive along the 32 km loop road through old colonial coffee and pepper plantations. Lunch by the central lake before the big descent. This road encircles the entire Yercaud plateau.
💡 Try the coffee grown locally — Yercaud's arabica estates are some of Tamil Nadu's best.

Pre-Departure

Final Preparation Checklist

🔧
Brake Inspection
130 combined hairpins will heavily load your brakes. Check brake pad thickness and fluid level before departure. Don't skip this — Valparai's descent after Kolli Hills will punish worn pads.
🛞
Tyre Pressure & Condition
Check all four tyres including the spare. Mountain roads amplify any existing tyre weakness. Inflate to the manufacturer's recommended ghat-driving pressure (usually lower than highway).
💧
Coolant & Engine Oil
Long mountain climbs in low gears heat engines significantly. Top up coolant and verify oil level. Carry 1L spare engine oil as backup.
🪪
TN E-Pass Registration
Register for free at epass.tnega.org before departure. May be required at Pollachi border checkpost. Takes 5 minutes online.
🚫
No Plastic — Zero Tolerance
Both Valparai and Kolli Hills are strictly monitored forest zones. Replace all plastic bottles with steel/glass. Forest officers conduct checks; fines are significant.
⛽
Full Tank at Every City
Fuel stations on Valparai and Kolli Hills hilltops are unreliable. Always fill up completely at Pollachi before the climb and Salem before the highway sprint home.
🥾
Trekking Footwear
Agaya Gangai falls requires 1,000 steps down and back up. The steps can be wet and slippery from spray. Proper grip shoes are non-negotiable.
🌡️
Layered Clothing
Valparai nights drop to ~16°C. Kolli Hills plateau is breezy and cool even in summer. A light fleece and windbreaker will be used every night of this trip.
📡
Offline Maps Downloaded
Mobile signal is patchy on all three ghats. Download Google Maps offline for Valparai, Kolli Hills, and Yercaud regions before departure.
